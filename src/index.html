Holding tabs (bridges)

 in material left under the tool

            
A part cut all the way through comes loose, lifts on the cutter and usually breaks. A tab is a short bridge left joining it to the sheet, snapped or sanded off afterwards. Tabs show on the toolpath in blue, and any part can override this in its own Cut panel. A part cut on the line never gets them.

Keyboard

        

        
at once if nothing longer starts the same way, otherwise after a short pause

        
Type a command and press Enter or
          Space; Esc clears it. Space still pans while nothing
          is typed, and Enter still finishes a polyline. Several aliases can share a command — separate them
          with commas.

        
Coordinates can be typed the same way:
          12,5 is an absolute point, @5,3 is measured from the
          last point, and @5<45 gives it as a distance and an angle. Numbers are in
          whichever unit is showing. Typing a comma switches to a coordinate even while a shape is being
          drawn, so 10,5 works on its own; #10,5 still does
          the same thing if you prefer to be explicit.
